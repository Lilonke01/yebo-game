YEBO - PLAY ON THIS COMPUTER

Double-click "Start Yebo" on your desktop, or "Start Yebo.cmd" in this folder.
The game opens in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:4173/

No internet connection or Codex session is needed to play. The voice recordings
and pictures are stored in this folder. Keep the audio and runtime folders here.
Progress is saved in the browser on this computer. Use the same browser and
address each time to keep your progress.

The local server is accessible only from this computer. Restart the launcher
after restarting Windows. Closing the browser does not erase progress.
