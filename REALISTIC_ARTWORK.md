# Realistic artwork

The numeracy and literacy illustrations now share locally bundled 3D-rendered characters, a landscape background and 27 realistic props in `assets3d/`.

Props include vehicles, perched and flying birds, a dog, a tree, a bicycle, a wrapped lollipop, a pedestrian signal, an apple, a bench, a stone, a plate, a blue bag, a cat, an umbrella, a teddy, a seed, pots at three growth stages, a watering can, a shop, a puddle, a drain, a football and a coin teaching token.

Objects are placed individually in code so quantities stay exact. The three- and four-tomato bowls remain separately verified images. Mathematical symbols, letters, labels, counters, arrows and interface icons remain crisp UI elements.

The blank metallic coin is an educational token; the denomination labels are rendered by the game. It is not a photograph or replica of South African currency. The supplied YearBeyond logo is unchanged.

All new artwork was generated with the image-generation skill during this project. The game references local PNG files and needs no image service or internet connection during play. Source-generation paths are retained in `assets3d/manifest.json` for maintenance; that file is not served by the game.

`art-review.html` is a local QA gallery. The normal game starts at `/`.
