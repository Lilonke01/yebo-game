## Interaction and evidence

One demonstration at the beginning of a fresh trip. Afterwards, one scene and one activity per screen, with optional hints, slow cached narration and varied claps. Search spots support taps and horizontal swipes. The robot is a five-second model, not a real-world crossing signal; the story explicitly requires a grown-up and a clear road.

English, Afrikaans and isiZulu narration use the existing consistent voice packs. Emoji decorations are removed from speech. First-language review remains recommended for the prototype translations.

Guided actions (packing, timed waiting, following a number path, distribution and searching) are marked as guided practice rather than independent answers. Hints and retries are recorded without penalties. The grown-up view offers a separate numeracy CSV.
